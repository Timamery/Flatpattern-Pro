The panel everything else is corrected relative to when panels taper — pick the one that stays flat/fixed once the part is folded (usually the widest one). The two ends of the chain get mitered so they fold flush with this panel; only matters when there's a taper.
Controls how the overall taper is distributed along the part: "Left" puts zero taper-induced slant on every panel before the Base Panel, with the whole difference absorbed after it; "Right" is the mirror; "Symmetric" splits it evenly between the two ends (the default). This affects the flat pattern and DXF directly, not just how the Bent Profile and 3D views are laid out.

Corner Treatment

Convex rounds the outer corner off (standard filleted corner). Concave scallops a quarter-circle notch into the corner instead. Applies to the 4 outer corners of the blank — start of panel 1 and end of the last panel.

Bend Reliefs

Optional relief notches cut into the top/bottom edge at each bend line, to stop the flange tearing at the fold.
